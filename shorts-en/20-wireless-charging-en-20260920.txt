How wireless charging transfers energy

Wireless chargers transfer energy between two coils placed close together.
Changing current in the charger's coil creates a changing magnetic field.
Changing magnetic flux through the phone's coil induces an electrical voltage.
Circuits inside the phone convert that electrical power for charging its battery.
Misalignment or extra distance reduces how strongly the two coils couple.
Position and spacing affect efficiency, and some supplied energy becomes heat.
