Why two window panes insulate

Double glazing uses trapped gas between two panes to slow heat transfer.
Heat travels through the inner glass, the gap, and the outer glass.
Air or argon in that gap conducts heat much less effectively than glass.
An excessively wide gap can allow circulation, increasing heat transfer by convection.
A low-emissivity coating also reduces heat transfer through infrared radiation.
Gap width, gas type, and coatings matter alongside the number of panes.
