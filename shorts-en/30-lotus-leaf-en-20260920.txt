Why water rolls off lotus leaves

Water beads up on lotus leaves and can roll away easily.
Their surfaces combine tiny bumps with waxy structures that resist wetting.
Air trapped beneath a droplet reduces the area actually touching solid material.
With little contact-line pinning, droplets can move easily when the leaf tilts.
Dirt particles that attach to rolling droplets can be carried away with them.
This behavior comes from both the microscopic texture and the surface chemistry.
