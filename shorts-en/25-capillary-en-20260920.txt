Why water climbs a narrow tube

Water can rise above the surrounding surface inside a narrow, clean glass tube.
Attraction to the glass makes water wet the wall and form a curved meniscus.
Surface tension along the contact line provides an upward force on the water.
As the column rises, its growing weight eventually balances that upward force.
Under the same conditions, a narrower tube supports a taller water column.
Changing the liquid or tube material changes the surface shape and height.
