A late bus picks up the passengers who arrived during its extra delay. More boarding makes it later still. The following bus finds fewer people waiting, so it catches up.

Our original illustrative simulation uses a circular route, steady passenger arrivals, fixed boarding times and no overtaking. All displayed timings and growth rates describe those assumptions. They are not measured predictions for a particular city. Holding a bus that is too close to the one ahead can help restore spacing; the result depends on the route, demand and control policy.
