Why rotating a filter blocks light

Two polarizing filters can turn nearly dark when you rotate one.
Light's electric field vibrates perpendicular to the direction the light travels.
The first filter transmits the component aligned with its transmission axis.
Align the second filter, and that polarized light passes through easily.
Turn its axis perpendicular to the first, and very little light gets through.
Polarized sunglasses use this effect to reduce glare reflected from water or roads.
