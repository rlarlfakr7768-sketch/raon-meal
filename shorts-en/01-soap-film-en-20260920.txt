Why soap bubbles change color

A soap bubble gets its colors from overlapping reflections.
Some light reflects off the front surface, while some returns from the back.
Their different paths strengthen some colors and weaken others when the waves overlap.
A small change in film thickness changes which colors reflect strongly.
As liquid drains downward, changing thickness makes those colored patterns move.
Changing your viewing angle also changes the paths, shifting the colors you see.
