How a transformer changes voltage

A transformer changes voltage through a changing magnetic field shared by two coils.
Alternating current in the first coil changes magnetic flux through the core.
That changing flux also passes through the second coil, inducing a voltage.
In an ideal transformer, the turns ratio determines the voltage ratio.
At the higher voltage, the same transferred power corresponds to a smaller current.
Unchanging direct current cannot sustain this induction because the magnetic flux stays constant.
