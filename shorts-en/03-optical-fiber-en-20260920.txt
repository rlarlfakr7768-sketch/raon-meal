How light follows an optical fiber

Light can travel along a fiber by repeatedly reflecting inside it.
The central core has a higher refractive index than the surrounding cladding.
At sufficiently large incidence angles, light reflects completely back into the core.
This repeated total internal reflection guides light along the fiber.
A gentle bend can preserve the conditions needed for that reflection.
A sharp bend lets some light escape, weakening the transmitted signal.
