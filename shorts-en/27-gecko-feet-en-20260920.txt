How gecko feet grip glass

Gecko toes carry tiny hairs that make very close contact with surfaces.
Each hair branches into even smaller tips, increasing the area of close contact.
At extremely short distances, weak attractions act between nearby molecules.
Across many contact points, those tiny forces add up enough to support the animal.
Changing toe angle peels the contacts away progressively, allowing the foot to release.
Inspired artificial surfaces also depend on conditions such as dust, moisture, and surface texture.
