How a heat pump warms a home

A heat pump uses electricity to move outdoor heat into your home.
Cold refrigerant in the outdoor coil absorbs heat from the surrounding air.
A compressor squeezes the refrigerant vapor, raising its pressure and temperature.
The hot refrigerant releases heat indoors and condenses into liquid.
An expansion device lowers its pressure before it returns to the outdoor coil.
Indoor heating includes both energy collected outside and energy supplied to the compressor.
