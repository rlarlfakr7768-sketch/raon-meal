Why hot roads look wet

A sun-heated road can look wet even when its surface is dry.
Air close to the hot road becomes less dense than the air above.
That density gradient also creates a gradual change in refractive index.
Light from the sky can curve through these layers and reach your eyes.
Your eyes trace the arriving rays backward along straight lines, placing the image below.
This image of the sky resembles a reflection from water: a road mirage.
