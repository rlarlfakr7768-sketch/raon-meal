How reflectors send light back

A bicycle reflector sends incoming light back toward its source.
On a flat mirror, the reflection angle equals the incoming angle.
Arrange mirrors at right angles, and light reflects more than once.
Three perpendicular faces reverse all three components of the light's direction.
The outgoing beam then travels almost opposite to the incoming beam.
Tiny repeated corner structures look bright to drivers sitting near their headlights.
