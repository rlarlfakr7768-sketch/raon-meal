Why tempered glass breaks into pieces

Thermally tempered glass breaks differently because manufacturing leaves stresses inside it.
Rapidly cooling the surface of hot glass makes its outer layers solidify first.
The cooling interior contracts, leaving surface compression and tension deeper inside.
Surface compression helps prevent small scratches from opening into larger cracks.
A deep enough crack can release stored elastic energy and branch rapidly.
The glass then breaks into many small pieces, which can still cause injury.
