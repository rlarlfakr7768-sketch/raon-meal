Why a bathroom mirror fogs

A bathroom mirror fogs because tiny water droplets form on its surface.
Warm, humid air cools as it reaches the colder mirror.
Below the dew point, some water vapor condenses into liquid water.
The small droplets refract and reflect light in many different directions.
That scattered light blurs the clear image you normally see in the mirror.
Warming the mirror or reducing humidity can reduce the condensation.
