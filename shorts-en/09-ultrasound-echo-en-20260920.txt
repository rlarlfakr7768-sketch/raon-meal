How an echo measures distance

An ultrasonic sensor measures distance by timing a returning sound pulse.
It sends a short pulse that travels outward through the air.
Some sound reflects from an object and returns to the sensor.
Multiply the round-trip time by sound speed to find the total distance traveled.
The object's distance is half that value, and temperature affects sound speed.
Tilted or absorbent surfaces can weaken the returning echo, making measurement harder.
