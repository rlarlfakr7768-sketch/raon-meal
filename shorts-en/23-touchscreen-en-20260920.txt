How a screen locates your finger

A capacitive touchscreen detects electrical changes when your finger approaches it.
Transparent electrodes form a grid beneath the screen's outer surface.
Electric fields form between electrodes, and capacitance describes their charge-storage behavior.
A conducting finger changes the electric field and the measured capacitance nearby.
Electronics compare changes across the grid to calculate the touch position.
Thick insulating gloves can weaken this coupling and make touch detection harder.
