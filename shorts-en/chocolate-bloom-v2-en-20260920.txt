Why chocolate turns white

Those pale streaks on chocolate are called chocolate bloom.
Chocolate contains sugar and cocoa particles packed into cocoa butter.
Changing storage temperatures can let fat migrate and crystallize at the surface.
These surface crystals scatter light, giving the chocolate a cloudy appearance.
Moisture can dissolve surface sugar, which forms new crystals as the water dries.
Fat and sugar bloom can occur together, so color alone can't tell them apart.
